Population structure of group B Streptococcus from semen reveals virulent and resistant lineages with implications for surveillance.

Merces, Ana Clarisse; Costa, Natalia Silva; Oliveira, Laura Maria Andrade; Botelho, Ana Caroline Nunes; Barros, Laylla Graca; Silva, Luisa de Miranda Basto; Teixeira, Lucia Martins; Fracalanzza, Sergio Eduardo Longo; Khan, Uzma; Bentley, Stephen; Jamrozy, Dorota; Pinto, Tatiana Castro Abreu
Microbial genomics · 2026-11-13 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究分析精液来源B族链球菌的群体结构、耐药与CRISPR多样性，CRISPR仅作为分型标记，未涉及切割/碱基编辑机制，与本课题无关。

摘要

Group B Streptococcus (GBS) is a major agent of perinatal infections. Although extensively studied as a pathogen in pregnant individuals and neonates, little is known about GBS from male adults, despite occasional reports of its occurrence in semen. We characterized 120 GBS isolates obtained from semen samples in Brazil between 2017 and 2022 to determine serotypes, antimicrobial susceptibility profile, virulence-associated determinants, clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic repeats (CRISPR) diversity and phylogenetic relationships with isolates from other clinical sources. Serotype V was the most frequent, followed by Ia, Ib, III, II and IV. Resistance was observed for tetracycline (84.1%), erythromycin (30.8%), levofloxacin (8.3%) and 12.5% exhibited multidrug resistance (MDR). The most common sequence types (STs) were ST1, ST144 and ST19. The isolates clustered into five major clonal complexes (CC1, CC17, CC19, CC23 and CC24) and six singletons. Serotypes, antimicrobial resistance and virulence-associated determinants markers varied across CCs. Resistance determinants detected included tetM, mefA, msrD, ermA and ermB. Levofloxacin-resistant isolates contained mutations in gyrA and/or parC and were predominantly linked to the serotype V/ST19 lineage. All isolates except one carried at least one alpha-like protein (Alp) gene. Phylogenetic analysis of isolates from semen, rectovaginal samples, urine and invasive infections showed that isolates clustered according to CC regardless of clinical source. CRISPR1 analysis revealed intra-clonal diversity, as isolates within the same CC displayed different CRISPR compositions. These findings indicate that GBS isolates recovered from semen belong to established GBS lineages also identified in other clinical sources and support the co-circulation of GBS lineages across different human niches.

摘要翻译

B族链球菌（Group B Streptococcus, GBS）是围产期感染的主要病原体。尽管其在孕妇和新生儿中作为病原体已被广泛研究，但关于男性成人中GBS的了解甚少，尽管偶尔有其在精液中出现的报道。我们对2017年至2022年间巴西精液样本中获得的120株GBS分离株进行了表征，以确定血清型、抗菌药物敏感性谱、毒力相关决定因子、成簇规律间隔短回文重复序列（CRISPR）多样性及其与其他临床来源分离株的系统发育关系。血清型V最为常见，其次为Ia、Ib、III、II和IV。观察到对四环素（84.1%）、红霉素（30.8%）、左氧氟沙星（8.3%）的耐药性，且12.5%表现出多重耐药（MDR）。最常见的序列型（STs）为ST1、ST144和ST19。这些分离株聚类为五个主要克隆复合体（CC1、CC17、CC19、CC23和CC24）及六个单例。血清型、抗菌药物耐药性和毒力相关决定因子标记在不同CC间存在差异。检测到的耐药决定因子包括tetM、mefA、msrD、ermA和ermB。左氧氟沙星耐药分离株含有gyrA和/或parC突变，并主要与血清型V/ST19谱系相关。除一株外，所有分离株至少携带一个α样蛋白（Alp）基因。对来自精液、直肠阴道样本、尿液和侵袭性感染的分离株进行系统发育分析显示，分离株根据CC聚类，与临床来源无关。CRISPR1分析揭示了克隆内多样性，因为同一CC内的分离株表现出不同的CRISPR组成。这些发现表明，从精液中回收的GBS分离株属于已建立的GBS谱系，这些谱系也见于其他临床来源，并支持GBS谱系在不同人类生态位中的共同循环。
