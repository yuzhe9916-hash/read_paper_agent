A comprehensive review of mixed-species biofilm infections and innovative therapeutic strategies.

Nazari, Mohsen; Kardan, Romina; Ahmadi, Amjad; Abolhasani, Fatemeh Sadat; Hemmati, Jaber
Molecular biology reports · 2026-10-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

综述混合物种生物膜感染及治疗策略，仅提及CRISPR-Cas作为抗菌手段之一，未涉及碱基编辑或交错切割机制，与本课题核心无关。

摘要

Mixed-species biofilm infections are important causes of persistent and difficult-to-treat infections. Compared with monospecies biofilms, mixed-species communities contain microorganisms with different physiological and genetic characteristics that interact through metabolic cooperation, chemical signaling, competition, and modification of the local environment. These interactions, together with the EPS matrix, physiological heterogeneity, and HGT, can increase antimicrobial tolerance, contributing to treatment failure and infection recurrence. Polymicrobial biofilms are associated with chronic wounds, respiratory infections, catheter- and device-associated infections, and other persistent infections. However, treatment of polymicrobial biofilms remains challenging because their complex structure and interspecies interactions can reduce antimicrobial efficacy. This review summarizes the clinical significance, formation, and persistence of mixed-species biofilms and discusses recent therapeutic strategies for their control. We also discuss recent approaches for controlling polymicrobial biofilms, including natural compounds, QS inhibition, CRISPR-Cas-based approaches, biofilm matrix targeting, metabolic and nutrient disruption, nanotechnology, and phage therapy. Recent studies show that these approaches can inhibit biofilm formation, disrupt established biofilms, enhance antimicrobial activity, and selectively target specific microbial populations. However, their effectiveness varies with microbial composition, biofilm maturity, matrix characteristics, and treatment conditions. Overall, current evidence supports the development of targeted and combination-based strategies for mixed-species biofilms. Further studies using clinically relevant models are needed to exactly define treatment efficacy and support the translation of these approaches into clinical practice.

摘要翻译

混合物种生物膜感染是持续性和难治性感染的重要原因。与单一物种生物膜相比，混合物种群落包含具有不同生理和遗传特征的微生物，这些微生物通过代谢合作、化学信号传导、竞争以及局部环境修饰进行相互作用。这些相互作用，连同EPS基质、生理异质性和HGT，可增强抗菌药物耐受性，导致治疗失败和感染复发。多种微生物生物膜与慢性伤口、呼吸道感染、导管及设备相关感染以及其他持续性感染相关。然而，由于多种微生物生物膜的复杂结构和种间相互作用可能降低抗菌效果，其治疗仍具挑战性。本综述总结了混合物种生物膜的临床意义、形成和持续性，并讨论了近期用于其控制的治疗策略。我们还探讨了控制多种微生物生物膜的最新方法，包括天然化合物、QS抑制、基于CRISPR-Cas的方法、生物膜基质靶向、代谢和营养干扰、纳米技术以及噬菌体疗法。近期研究表明，这些方法可抑制生物膜形成、破坏已建立的生物膜、增强抗菌活性，并选择性靶向特定微生物群体。然而，其有效性随微生物组成、生物膜成熟度、基质特征和治疗条件而变化。总体而言，当前证据支持针对混合物种生物膜开发靶向和联合策略。需要进一步使用临床相关模型的研究，以精确确定治疗效果并支持这些方法向临床实践的转化。
