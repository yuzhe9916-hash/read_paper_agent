Structural basis for tetraspanin-dependent surface export and adhesive function of integrin alpha3beta1.

Litzau, Lauren; Wren, Elizabeth K; Kruse, Andrew C; Blacklow, Stephen C
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-10-25 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究通过冷冻电镜解析整合素与四旋蛋白复合物结构，仅使用CRISPR作为基因敲除工具，不涉及切割机制或碱基编辑，与本课题无关。

摘要

Integrin alpha3beta1 (ITGalpha3beta1) is a member of an integrin subfamily that binds to laminin proteins and promotes attachment of epithelial cells to the basement membrane. ITGalpha3beta1 forms a complex with the tetraspanin CD151, and loss-of-function mutations in both ITGalpha3 and CD151 cause epidermolysis bullosa, a severe skin blistering disease resulting from a defect in basement membrane attachment. Here, we report the cryoEM structure of an ITGalpha3beta1 complex with CD151 and show that mutation of CD151 at the binding interface disrupts complex formation in cells. Strikingly, CRISPR-mediated knockout of CD151 leads to a variably penetrant ITGalpha3beta1 surface export defect that is restored by re-expression of wild-type but not interface-mutated CD151. Together, these studies define the molecular basis for binding of CD151 to ITGalpha3beta1, and show that CD151 promotes ITGalpha3beta1 surface export, providing a biochemical explanation for the CD151 loss-of-function phenotype in epidermolysis bullosa.

摘要翻译

整合素α3β1（ITGalpha3beta1）是整合素亚家族的一员，该亚家族与层粘连蛋白结合并促进上皮细胞附着于基底膜。ITGalpha3beta1与四旋蛋白CD151形成复合物，且ITGalpha3和CD151的功能缺失突变均可导致大疱性表皮松解症，这是一种因基底膜附着缺陷引起的严重皮肤起疱疾病。在此，我们报道了ITGalpha3beta1与CD151复合物的冷冻电镜结构，并表明在结合界面处突变CD151会破坏细胞中的复合物形成。引人注目的是，CRISPR介导的CD151敲除导致一种可变外显率的ITGalpha3beta1表面输出缺陷，该缺陷可通过野生型CD151的重新表达得以恢复，而界面突变的CD151则不能。综合来看，这些研究定义了CD151与ITGalpha3beta1结合的分子基础，并表明CD151促进ITGalpha3beta1的表面输出，为CD151功能缺失表型在大疱性表皮松解症中的表现提供了生化解释。
