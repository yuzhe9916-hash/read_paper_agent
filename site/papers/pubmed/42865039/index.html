Genome-wide CRISPR screens identify shared and context-specific regulators of podocyte resistance to oxidative injury.

Zhen, Xuan; Hu, Jiaying; Wang, Xiao; Qing, Jianbo; Wu, Junnan
Renal failure · 2026-10-13 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究利用CRISPR-Cas9全基因组筛选鉴定足细胞氧化应激损伤的调控基因，但仅涉及CRISPR作为筛选工具，未研究切割/碱基编辑分子机制，与本课题核心机制无关。

摘要

Podocyte injury is a convergent driver of glomerular disease progression, yet the genetic determinants that govern podocyte vulnerability to oxidative stress remain incompletely defined. Here, we performed genome-wide CRISPR-Cas9 knockout screens in differentiated human podocytes exposed to two complementary injury models: Adriamycin (ADR), a clinically relevant inducer of podocyte damage, and hydrogen peroxide (H(2)O(2)), a direct oxidative stress challenge. Human podocytes were transduced with a genome-wide sgRNA library, differentiated under non-permissive conditions and subjected to optimized stress conditions that imposed selective pressure while preserving library complexity. Across five independent replicates per condition, sequencing-based sgRNA profiling demonstrated high reproducibility, balanced library representation and clear treatment-specific selection. The ADR screen identified 819 depleted and 456 enriched genes, whereas the H(2)O(2) screen identified 277 depleted and 215 enriched genes. Integrated analysis revealed both model-specific dependencies and 27 shared depleted genes across the two injury conditions, implicating stress adaptation, chromatin regulation, mitochondrial metabolism, inflammatory signaling and cytoskeletal organization as candidate processes required for podocyte survival. These genome-wide screens provide a functional framework for prioritizing genes that regulate podocyte resilience to oxidative injury and offer a resource for mechanistic studies of glomerular disease.

摘要翻译

足细胞损伤是肾小球疾病进展的的汇聚性驱动因素，然而调控足细胞对氧化应激易感性的遗传决定因素仍未完全明确。在此，我们在分化的人足细胞中进行了全基因组CRISPR-Cas9敲除筛选，暴露于两种互补的损伤模型：阿霉素（ADR），一种临床相关的足细胞损伤诱导剂，以及过氧化氢（H(2)O(2)），一种直接的氧化应激挑战。人足细胞被转导全基因组sgRNA文库，在非允许条件下分化，并经受优化的应激条件，该条件施加选择压力同时保持文库复杂性。在每个条件五个独立重复中，基于测序的sgRNA分析显示出高重现性、平衡的文库表示和明确的处理特异性选择。ADR筛选鉴定出819个缺失和456个富集基因，而H(2)O(2)筛选鉴定出277个缺失和215个富集基因。整合分析揭示了模型特异性依赖性和两种损伤条件下27个共享缺失基因，暗示应激适应、染色质调控、线粒体代谢、炎症信号传导和细胞骨架组织作为足细胞存活所需的候选过程。这些全基因组筛选为优先排序调控足细胞对氧化损伤抵抗力的基因提供了功能框架，并为肾小球疾病的机制研究提供了资源。
