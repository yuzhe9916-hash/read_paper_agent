Type III Effectors of Pseudomonas syringae pv. tomato DC3000: Molecular Mechanisms, Host Manipulation Networks, and Implications for Plant Disease Resistance.

Zhong, Xiaoju; Huang, Jianzhong
Life (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026-10-27 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文综述丁香假单胞菌III型效应子的分子机制与宿主操控网络，虽提及CRISPR编辑用于抗病育种，但核心对象非CRISPR/碱基编辑/交错切割，机制无可迁移启发。

摘要

Pseudomonas syringae pv. tomato DC3000 (Pst DC3000) is a reference system for dissecting how type III-secreted effectors (T3Es) suppress plant immunity and reconfigure host physiology. Here, we reassess the DC3000 effector repertoire through an evidence-graded framework that links experimentally resolved effector-target mechanisms to repertoire-level genetic interactions and, ultimately, to disease-resistance strategies. We distinguish findings demonstrated with Pst DC3000 from mechanistic examples derived from other P. syringae pathovars and from computationally generated hypotheses. At the molecular level, validated DC3000 effectors perturb pattern-recognition receptor signaling, protein turnover, vesicle trafficking, plasmodesmatal communication, organelle function, and hormone-associated processes. At the repertoire level, deletion and reconstruction studies reveal functional redundancy, specialization, conditional suppression, and context-dependent interactions; importantly, convergence on a common host phenotype is not treated as evidence of direct physical cooperation between effectors. We further evaluate how effectoromics, multi-omics, single-cell and spatial approaches, structure prediction, and machine-learning tools can prioritize new hypotheses while emphasizing their need for genetic, biochemical, and infection-based validation. Finally, we consider how conserved effector targets, susceptibility factors, NLR engineering, and CRISPR-based editing may translate mechanistic knowledge into more durable bacterial-disease resistance. This synthesis positions the DC3000 system as a bridge between mechanistic effector biology, quantitative effector-network analysis, and experimentally testable strategies for crop protection.

摘要翻译

丁香假单胞菌番茄致病变种DC3000（Pst DC3000）是解析三型分泌效应子（T3Es）如何抑制植物免疫并重构宿主生理的参考系统。在此，我们通过一个证据分级框架重新评估DC3000效应子库，该框架将实验解析的效应子-靶标机制与效应子库水平的遗传互作，并最终与抗病策略联系起来。我们区分了用Pst DC3000证实的发现与源自其他P. syringae致病变种及计算生成假设的机制性示例。在分子水平上，已验证的DC3000效应子干扰模式识别受体信号传导、蛋白质周转、囊泡运输、胞间连丝通讯、细胞器功能及激素相关过程。在效应子库水平上，缺失和重建研究揭示了功能冗余、特化、条件性抑制及情境依赖性互作；重要的是，对共同宿主表型的趋同并不被视为效应子间直接物理合作的证据。我们进一步评估了效应子组学、多组学、单细胞和空间方法、结构预测及机器学习工具如何优先化新假设，同时强调它们需要遗传学、生物化学和基于感染的验证。最后，我们考虑了保守效应子靶标、易感因子、NLR工程及CRISPR-based编辑如何将机制性知识转化为更持久的细菌性病害抗性。本综述将DC3000系统定位为连接机制性效应子生物学、定量效应子网络分析和可实验验证的作物保护策略的桥梁。
