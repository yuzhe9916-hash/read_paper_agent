Functional characterization of os9 reveals its role in zebrafish developmental regulation.

Xie, Qiuting; Wu, Lingxin; Wu, Jiayi; He, Meiling; Li, Wenhua
Gene · 2026-11-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究利用CRISPR/Cas9敲除斑马鱼os9基因，表征其在发育中的ERAD功能，但仅将CRISPR作为基因敲除工具，未涉及切割机制或碱基编辑，与本课题核心机制无关。

摘要

Endoplasmic reticulum (ER)-associated degradation (ERAD) plays a critical role in maintaining proteostasis during development, yet its in vivo functions remain incompletely understood. Here, we investigated the role of os9, a key ERAD component, in zebrafish development. Sequence and protein-protein interaction analyses revealed that Os9 is highly conserved across vertebrates and participates in ER protein quality control. Using CRISPR/Cas9, we generated os9 knockout zebrafish lines and found that loss of os9 leads to developmental defects with incomplete penetrance. Affected os9 mutant larvae exhibited cardiovascular abnormalities, including pericardial edema, disrupted cardiac structure, and abnormal erythrocyte distribution. In addition, affected os9 mutants displayed microphthalmia with reduced retinal size, while retinal lamination was largely preserved. Ultrastructural analysis revealed pronounced ER dilation and mitochondrial disruption in photoreceptor cells, accompanied by downregulation of photoreceptor marker genes and impaired photoreceptor development. Furthermore, os9 deficiency was associated with increased apoptosis and oxidative stress, as evidenced by elevated Caspase 9 activity, altered expression of apoptosis-related genes, and imbalance of antioxidant markers. Transcriptome analysis of affected os9 mutants revealed extensive gene expression reprogramming, characterized by enrichment of stress- and apoptosis-related pathways and suppression of metabolic and redox-associated processes. Notably, genes involved in protein processing in ER were significantly upregulated, while pathways related to retinol metabolism and phototransduction were downregulated. Collectively, these findings suggest that os9 is required for maintaining cellular homeostasis during zebrafish development and that its loss leads to ER stress, oxidative stress, apoptosis, and tissue-specific developmental defects, particularly in the cardiovascular system and retina.

摘要翻译

内质网（ER）相关降解（ERAD）在发育过程中对维持蛋白质稳态起着关键作用，然而其体内功能仍未完全阐明。在此，我们研究了os9（一个关键的ERAD组分）在斑马鱼发育中的作用。序列和蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用分析显示，Os9在脊椎动物中高度保守，并参与内质网蛋白质质量控制。利用CRISPR/Cas9技术，我们生成了os9敲除斑马鱼品系，并发现os9缺失导致不完全外显的发育缺陷。受影响的os9突变体幼虫表现出心血管异常，包括心包水肿、心脏结构破坏和红细胞分布异常。此外，受影响的os9突变体表现出小眼症，视网膜尺寸减小，而视网膜分层基本保留。超微结构分析显示，感光细胞中内质网显著扩张和线粒体破坏，伴随感光细胞标记基因下调及感光细胞发育受损。进一步地，os9缺陷与细胞凋亡和氧化应激增加相关，这通过Caspase 9活性升高、凋亡相关基因表达改变以及抗氧化标记物失衡得以证实。对受影响的os9突变体的转录组分析揭示了广泛的基因表达重编程，其特征为应激和凋亡相关通路富集，以及代谢和氧化还原相关过程抑制。值得注意的是，参与内质网蛋白质加工的基因显著上调，而视黄醇代谢和光转导相关通路则下调。综合这些发现表明，os9在斑马鱼发育过程中对维持细胞稳态是必需的，其缺失导致内质网应激、氧化应激、细胞凋亡以及组织特异性发育缺陷，尤其是在心血管系统和视网膜中。
