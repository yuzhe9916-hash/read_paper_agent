CRISPR/Cas12a-Based Aptasensor for Cas9 Protein Detection in Spiked Food Matrices.

Wang, Zizhao; Wang, Jing; Li, Ling; Chen, Guofeng; Wen, Hongtao; Liu, Baohai; Dong, Yiyang
Molecules (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026-10-27 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文开发基于CRISPR/Cas12a的荧光适配体传感器检测食品基质中的Cas9蛋白，属于分析检测工具应用，未涉及切割机制、交错断裂或碱基编辑的分子机制研究。

摘要

Residual gene editors are a concern associated with the widespread application of CRISPR/Cas9 technology. Here, we developed a CRISPR/Cas12a-based fluorescent aptasensor for the qualitative and quantitative detection of Cas9 protein. Secondary structure prediction and molecular docking were combined to identify potential binding sites of the Cas9-specific aptamer. A fluorescence aptasensor was constructed based on competitive binding: in the presence of Cas9, the aptamer binds to the target and releases complementary DNA (cDNA), which activates the trans-cleavage activity of Cas12a. Cas12a subsequently cleaves a single-stranded DNA (ssDNA) reporter labeled with a fluorophore and a quencher, generating a fluorescence signal, expressed in relative fluorescence units (RFU), that increases with Cas9 concentration. The optimized conditions comprised a trans-cleavage reaction time of 20 min, a Cas12a:CRISPR RNA (crRNA) molar ratio of 1:1, a final aptamer concentration of 180 nM, and a combination of low- and high-salt buffers to balance molecular recognition and protein stability. The proposed assay enabled Cas9 protein detection in buffer and Cas9-spiked soybean milk and cereal matrices. These results represent a proof-of-concept evaluation in complex food matrices rather than validation of authentic residual Cas9 detection. Further studies using unspiked gene-edited samples with independently verified Cas9 residues and an orthogonal reference method are required.

摘要翻译

残留基因编辑蛋白是与CRISPR/Cas9技术广泛应用相关的一个担忧。在此，我们开发了一种基于CRISPR/Cas12a的荧光适配体传感器，用于Cas9蛋白的定性和定量检测。结合二级结构预测与分子对接 (molecular docking) 来识别Cas9特异性适配体的潜在结合位点。基于竞争性结合构建了一种荧光适配体传感器：在Cas9存在时，适配体与靶标结合并释放互补DNA (cDNA)，从而激活Cas12a的反式切割活性。Cas12a随后切割标记有荧光基团和淬灭基团的单链DNA (ssDNA) 报告探针，产生荧光信号，以相对荧光单位 (RFU) 表示，该信号随Cas9浓度增加而增强。优化条件包括反式切割反应时间为20 min、Cas12a:CRISPR RNA (crRNA) 摩尔比为1:1、适配体终浓度为180 nM，以及采用低盐和高盐缓冲液组合以平衡分子识别与蛋白质稳定性。所提出的检测方法能够实现缓冲液以及加标Cas9的豆奶和谷物基质中的Cas9蛋白检测。这些结果代表了在复杂食品基质中的概念验证性评估，而非对真实残留Cas9检测的验证。需要使用未经加标的基因编辑样本、经独立验证的Cas9残留以及正交参考方法开展进一步研究。
