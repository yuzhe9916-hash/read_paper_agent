Structural basis and rational engineering of a thermophilic Cas13a for robust RNA detection.

Oladeji, Seye J; Gonzalez, Diego H; Deng, Xiangyu; You, Zheng; Zhang, Yuxuan; Golla, Devin A; Smith, Jamie; Zhang, Yi; Gao, Xue; Lillehoj, Peter B; Gao, Yang
Cell chemical biology · 2026-10-13 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究通过生化表征和结构指导工程化改造嗜热Cas13a，揭示其靶标识别与切割机制并显著提升RNA检测灵敏度，直接涉及CRISPR-Cas13a的蛋白-DNA/RNA互作与催化机制，对碱基编辑和交错切割机制研究具有方法学启发。

摘要

Cas13a enzymes are particularly attractive for nucleic acid detection due to their robust collateral activity. Recently, there has been a surge in the discovery and engineering of thermostable Cas13a proteins for nucleic acid detection. The thermostable Cas13a from Thermoclostridium caenicola (TccCas13a) was identified; however, its native target sensitivity and collateral activity are relatively low and require optimization for molecular diagnostic applications. Here, we report comprehensive biochemical characterization and structure-guided engineering of TccCas13a. Our analyses reveal its intrinsic thermostability, nucleotide substrate preferences, and tolerance to structured target RNA substrates. Rational engineering of the spacer-target RNA binding channel improved target engagement and enabled determination of a 3.13 A cryo-electron microscopy ternary structure. Furthermore, extensive structure-guided engineering of active site-proximal regions yielded a variant with over approximately 100-fold enhanced target sensitivity and collateral activity across diverse biological matrices. Together, our work provides mechanistic insights and establishes TccCas13a as a platform for next-generation RNA diagnostics.

摘要翻译

Cas13a酶因其强大的附带活性，在核酸检测领域尤为引人注目。近期，针对核酸检测的热稳定Cas13a蛋白的发现与工程化改造激增。已鉴定出源自Thermoclostridium caenicola的热稳定Cas13a（TccCas13a）；然而，其天然靶标敏感性和附带活性相对较低，需优化以满足分子诊断应用需求。在此，我们报告了对TccCas13a的全面生化表征及结构引导工程化改造。我们的分析揭示了其内在热稳定性、核苷酸底物偏好性，以及对结构化靶RNA底物的耐受性。对间隔区-靶RNA结合通道的理性工程化改造增强了靶标结合能力，并实现了3.13 Å冷冻电镜三元结构的解析。此外，对活性位点邻近区域进行广泛的结构引导工程化改造，产生了一个变体，其在多种生物基质中的靶标敏感性和附带活性增强了约100倍。综上，我们的工作提供了机制性见解，并将TccCas13a确立为下一代RNA诊断的平台。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：TccCas13a 虽具有内在热稳定性，但其天然靶标敏感性和 collateral activity 较低，不足以直接用于分子诊断。如何通过结构引导工程提升其检测性能？
  • 为什么重要：热稳定 Cas13a 在常温运输、现场检测（point-of-care）场景中具有独特优势；提升其灵敏度和 collateral activity 是将其推向实际诊断应用的关键瓶颈。
  • 现有方法为何不足：此前对 Cas13a 的工程改造多基于序列比对或随机突变，缺乏结构层面的精确指导；且对热稳定 Cas13a 的底物偏好和结构容忍度缺乏系统表征。
  • 精确研究问题：Can structure-guided engineering of the spacer-target RNA binding channel and active site-proximal regions of TccCas13a enhance its target sensitivity and collateral activity by ~100-fold across diverse biological matrices?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

  • 对 TccCas13a 进行系统生化表征（热稳定性、核苷酸底物偏好、结构化靶标 RNA 耐受性）。
  • 解析 3.13 Å cryo-EM 三元复合物结构（TccCas13a–crRNA–target RNA）。
  • 基于结构对 spacer-target RNA 结合通道和活性位点邻近区域进行理性突变。
  • 在多种生物基质中验证工程变体的检测性能。

2) 核心洞察

  • spacer-target RNA 结合通道是靶标识别效率的限速步骤：改造该通道可提升靶标结合亲和力，从而增强靶标敏感性。
  • 活性位点邻近区域的残基可显著影响 collateral activity：通过结构引导突变，可在不牺牲特异性的前提下大幅提升非特异性 RNA 切割活性。
  • 热稳定性与催化效率可同时优化：TccCas13a 的内在热稳定性为工程提供了稳健骨架，结构引导改造可叠加性能提升。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 对 Cas13 家族蛋白，底物结合通道的几何形状和残基组成是决定靶标识别效率的关键工程靶点，这一思路可迁移至 Cas13 碱基编辑器的靶标 RNA 结合优化。
  • 活性位点邻近区域的非催化残基可显著影响催化效率，提示在碱基编辑器中，脱氨酶结构域与 Cas13 的界面残基工程可能同样重要。
  • 结构引导工程优于随机突变：高分辨率结构可精确定位限速步骤，减少筛选工作量。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a potentially valuable contribution to the RNA diagnostics field, addressing a clear need for improved thermostable Cas13a variants. The combination of biochemical characterization, structure determination, and rational engineering is a coherent and ambitious approach. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the robustness of the claims. Key quantitative details, validation metrics for the cryo-EM structure, and the nature of the "approximately 100-fold" enhancement are not assessable from the supplied material. The work is likely of interest to the molecular diagnostics and CRISPR enzymology communities, but the strength of the conclusions cannot be verified at this stage.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in molecular diagnostics, particularly those developing point-of-care nucleic acid detection platforms; CRISPR enzymologists studying Cas13 structure-function relationships; and groups working on thermostable enzyme engineering for field-deployable diagnostics. The combination of structural data and functional enhancement would appeal to both fundamental and applied audiences.
  • Major strengths The work addresses a recognized limitation in the field, namely the relatively low activity of thermostable Cas13a variants. The structure-guided engineering approach is methodologically sound in principle. The reported cryo-EM structure at 3.13 Å, if properly validated, would be a valuable addition to the Cas13a structural repertoire. The claim of enhanced performance across diverse biological matrices suggests practical applicability.
