Structure-activity relationship and antibacterial mechanism of saponins from quinoa husks against Bacillus cereus and their application in rice.

Han, Chengcheng; Wang, Yan; Yang, Lijing; He, Yi; Song, Linmeng; Zhang, Ruoyu; Lu, Hao; Xue, Peng
Current research in food science · 2026-11-13 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究藜麦壳皂苷对蜡状芽孢杆菌的抗菌机制，虽涉及CRISPR-Cas9敲除但仅作为基因工具，与CRISPR/交错切割/碱基编辑的分子机制无关。

摘要

Bacillus cereus (B. cereus) is a major foodborne pathogen and a persistent food safety concern. Although plant saponins are promising natural antimicrobials, the antibacterial activity of Chenopodium quinoa Willd. husk saponins against B. cereus remains poorly understood. This study investigated the structure-activity relationship and antibacterial mechanism of quinoa husk saponins against B. cereus and evaluate their potential application in rice preservation. In this study, less polar saponins exhibited stronger antibacterial activity against B. cereus than more polar saponins among the twelve tested saponins. Among them, phytolaccagenic acid (Q8), isolated from quinoa husks for the first time, exhibited the strongest antibacterial activity, with minimum inhibitory concentration and minimum bactericidal concentration values of 125 and 1000 mug/mL, respectively. Q8 showed low cytotoxicity in GES-1 and effectively inhibited B. cereus proliferation and spoilage in rice. Further assays showed that Q8 disrupted membrane stability, causing membrane potential dissipation, protein leakage, cell deformation, and reactive oxygen species accumulation. Metabolomic and lipidomic analyses revealed Q8 mainly affected glutathione metabolism, cysteine and methionine metabolism, fatty acid degradation, and glycerophospholipid/glycerolipid metabolism. Molecular docking, RT-qPCR, CRISPR-Cas9 knockout, and genetic complementation analyses confirmed that squalene-hopene cyclase is essential for hopanoid biosynthesis, and hopanoids protect B. cereus against Q8-induced membrane damage. These findings support the potential application of quinoa saponins as natural antibacterial agents in food systems.

摘要翻译

蜡样芽孢杆菌（Bacillus cereus, B. cereus）是一种主要的食源性病原体，也是持续存在的食品安全隐患。尽管植物皂苷是有前景的天然抗菌剂，但藜麦（Chenopodium quinoa Willd.）壳皂苷对蜡样芽孢杆菌的抗菌活性仍知之甚少。本研究探讨了藜麦壳皂苷对蜡样芽孢杆菌的构效关系及抗菌机制，并评估其在稻米保鲜中的潜在应用。在本研究中，十二种测试皂苷中，极性较低的皂苷对蜡样芽孢杆菌表现出比极性较高的皂苷更强的抗菌活性。其中，首次从藜麦壳中分离出的商陆酸（phytolaccagenic acid, Q8）表现出最强的抗菌活性，其最低抑菌浓度和最低杀菌浓度值分别为125和1000 μg/mL。Q8在GES-1细胞中表现出低细胞毒性，并有效抑制稻米中蜡样芽孢杆菌的增殖和腐败。进一步实验表明，Q8破坏了膜稳定性，导致膜电位耗散、蛋白质泄漏、细胞变形和活性氧积累。代谢组学和脂质组学分析显示，Q8主要影响谷胱甘肽代谢、半胱氨酸和蛋氨酸代谢、脂肪酸降解以及甘油磷脂/甘油脂代谢。分子对接、RT-qPCR、CRISPR-Cas9敲除和遗传互补分析证实，角鲨烯- hopene环化酶对类何帕烷生物合成至关重要，且类何帕烷保护蜡样芽孢杆菌免受Q8诱导的膜损伤。这些发现支持藜麦皂苷作为食品系统中天然抗菌剂的潜在应用。
