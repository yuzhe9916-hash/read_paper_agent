MINOS1-Mediated Mitochondrial Remodeling: A Critical Regulator of Apoptosis in Hypoxic Intestinal Epithelium.

Yuan, Jianlin; Jian, Chuanhui; Li, Xiaokai; Li, Xuemin; Chi, Shaojie; Wang, Xun; Lu, Lu; Ge, Liangpeng; Li, Mingzhou; Long, Keren
Free radical biology & medicine · 2026-10-04 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用CRISPR-Cas9筛选鉴定MINOS1调控缺氧肠上皮凋亡的线粒体嵴重塑机制，虽以CRISPR为筛选工具但核心对象非碱基编辑/交错切割，机制聚焦线粒体凋亡而非核酸酶切割，仅筛选方法可迁移，整体关联度低。

摘要

Intestinal epithelial cells (IECs) are highly vulnerable to mitochondrial dysfunction and lethal apoptosis under severe hypoxic stress. However, the upstream genetic determinants governing mucosal redox resilience remain poorly defined. To investigate the mechanisms of intestinal epithelial cell apoptosis under cobalt chloride ()-induced hypoxic conditions, we established a -induced lethal hypoxia model in IPEC-J2 cells and performed a genome-wide CRISPR-Cas9 screen. This screen identified MINOS1 as an essential gene for tolerance to -induced hypoxic cell death. Systematic cell phenotyping and transcriptomic analyses revealed that exposure triggers robust intrinsic apoptosis and oxidative stress, whereas MINOS1 ablation remodels the basal transcriptomic landscape to establish an adaptive pro-survival state. Ultrastructural observation via transmission electron microscopy and subcellular functional assays demonstrated that MINOS1 deficiency eliminates canonical crista junctions (CJs) and induces distinctive concentric, "onion-like" remodeling of the inner mitochondrial membrane. Under severe hypoxia, MINOS1-knockout (KO) enterocytes retained stable mitochondrial membrane potential (DeltaPsim), suppressed pathological mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP) opening, sustained antioxidant enzyme (SOD and CAT) activities, and abrogated intracellular ROS accumulation and lipid peroxidation. Pharmacological intervention with the mitochondria-targeted antioxidant Mitoquinone mesylate precisely phenocopied the cytoprotective phenotype of MINOS1 ablation, confirming a mitochondria-dependent redox regulatory mechanism. Crucially, subcellular fractionation assays verified that remodeled concentric mitochondrial membranes form a unique topological barrier that physically sequesters cytochrome c within mitochondria, blocking its cytosolic release and subsequent activation of caspase-9 and caspase-3. Accordingly, MINOS1-deficient enterocytes tolerate extreme hypoxic stress by structurally uncoupling mitochondrial oxidative stress from downstream apoptotic execution. Collectively, these findings establish MINOS1-dependent cristae topology as a fundamental biophysical gatekeeper of intestinal mucosal redox homeostasis, providing a novel structure-targeted therapeutic strategy for hypoxia-associated intestinal disorders.

摘要翻译

肠上皮细胞（IECs）在严重低氧应激下极易发生线粒体功能障碍和致死性凋亡。然而，调控黏膜氧化还原耐受性的上游遗传决定因素仍不明确。为探究氯化钴（）诱导低氧条件下肠上皮细胞凋亡的机制，我们在IPEC-J2细胞中建立了诱导的致死性低氧模型，并进行了全基因组CRISPR-Cas9筛选。该筛选鉴定出MINOS1为耐受诱导低氧细胞死亡的关键基因。系统性细胞表型分析和转录组学分析揭示，暴露触发强烈的内源性凋亡和氧化应激，而MINOS1敲除重塑基础转录组景观，建立适应性促生存状态。通过透射电子显微镜的超微结构观察和亚细胞功能实验表明，MINOS1缺失消除典型嵴连接（CJs），并诱导线粒体内膜独特的同心圆状“洋葱样”重塑。在严重低氧条件下，MINOS1敲除（KO）肠细胞保持稳定的线粒体膜电位（DeltaPsim），抑制病理性线粒体通透性转换孔（mPTP）开放，维持抗氧化酶（SOD和CAT）活性，并消除细胞内ROS积累和脂质过氧化。线粒体靶向抗氧化剂Mitoquinone mesylate的药理学干预精确模拟了MINOS1缺失的细胞保护表型，证实了线粒体依赖性氧化还原调控机制。关键的是，亚细胞分级实验验证了重塑的同心圆状线粒体膜形成独特的拓扑屏障，物理性隔离线粒体内的细胞色素c，阻断其胞质释放及随后的caspase-9和caspase-3激活。因此，MINOS1缺陷肠细胞通过结构上解偶联线粒体氧化应激与下游凋亡执行，从而耐受极端低氧应激。综上，这些发现确立MINOS1依赖性嵴拓扑结构作为肠黏膜氧化还原稳态的基本生物物理门控因子，为低氧相关肠道疾病提供了一种新型结构靶向治疗策略。
