Aire deficiency disrupts thymic architecture but preserves TCRVbeta repertoire diversity while altering thymic output.

Matos, Adriana Arruda; Araujo, Itaua Leston; Munuera, Matheus Dominato; Palma, Patricia Viana Bonini; Bonaldo, Camila; Brito, Leticia Agrelli; Mendes-da-Cruz, Daniella Areas; Savino, Wilson; Passos, Geraldo Aleixo; Donadi, Eduardo Antonio
Immunology letters · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究探讨Aire缺陷对胸腺结构和T细胞输出的影响，不涉及无序蛋白、蛋白互作或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

The autoimmune regulator (Aire) gene is essential for the establishment of central tolerance by promoting the ectopic expression of tissue-restricted antigens in medullary thymic epithelial cells (mTECs). Although Aire deficiency impairs negative selection, its impact on thymic architecture, output, and early T cell development remains incompletely understood. Here, we used a murine Aire-deficient model carrying a CRISPR-Cas9-induced deletion within exon 6, disrupting the SAND domain, to investigate the consequences of Aire loss on thymic structure and function. Aire-deficient mice exhibited focal alopecia and marked disorganization of the thymic medulla. T cell receptor excision circle (TREC) analyses revealed altered peripheral TREC dynamics and a reduced sj/betaTREC ratio, indicating altered thymic output and impaired intrathymic precursor expansion. Complementary flow-cytometric analysis showed a significant reduction in the proportion of CD4(+)CD8(+) double-positive thymocytes in heterozygous mutants, supporting altered intrathymic maturation. Serum IL-27, IL-28B, and IL-31 were decreased in homozygous mutants, whereas IL-22 and IL-23 remained unchanged. Despite these alterations, TCRVbeta repertoire sequencing revealed no significant differences in rearrangements, clonality, or V/J gene usage. Together, these findings indicate that Aire deficiency disrupts thymic architecture and output, while preserving overall TCR repertoire diversity.

摘要翻译

自身免疫调节因子（Aire）基因通过促进髓质胸腺上皮细胞（mTECs）中组织限制性抗原的异位表达，对建立中枢耐受至关重要。尽管Aire缺陷会损害阴性选择，但其对胸腺结构、输出及早期T细胞发育的影响仍不完全清楚。在此，我们使用携带CRISPR-Cas9诱导的外显子6缺失（破坏SAND结构域）的鼠Aire缺陷模型，研究Aire缺失对胸腺结构和功能的影响。Aire缺陷小鼠表现出局灶性脱发和胸腺髓质的显著紊乱。T细胞受体切除环（TREC）分析显示外周TREC动力学改变以及sj/betaTREC比值降低，表明胸腺输出改变和胸腺内前体细胞扩增受损。补充的流式细胞术分析显示，杂合突变体中CD4(+)CD8(+)双阳性胸腺细胞的比例显著降低，支持胸腺内成熟过程的改变。纯合突变体血清中IL-27、IL-28B和IL-31水平降低，而IL-22和IL-23保持不变。尽管存在这些改变，TCRVbeta库测序显示重排、克隆性或V/J基因使用无显著差异。综合这些发现表明，Aire缺陷破坏了胸腺结构和输出，同时保留了整体TCR库多样性。
