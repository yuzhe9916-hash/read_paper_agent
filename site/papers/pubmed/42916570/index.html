High-Level Mycoprotein Production through Metabolic Regulation of Concanavalin A-Like Lectin/Glucanase Domain-Containing Protein in Fusarium venenatum.

Wu, Xiaohui; Luo, Shijun; Chen, Yaru; Du, Guocheng; Chen, Jian; Liu, Xiao
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究真菌蛋白生产菌株的代谢工程改造，与CRISPR/碱基编辑/交错切割机制无关，无可迁移的分子机制启发。

摘要

Mycoprotein produced by Fusarium venenatum is emerging as an increasingly widespread edible microbial protein source. However, their development is constrained by low protein content, inefficient substrate conversion, and substantial byproducts like ethanol, leading to considerable carbon loss. This study identifies a new genetic target FvCLG encoding concanavalin A-like lectin/glucanase domain-containing protein that associated with cell wall construction. In the 5 L fermenter, the DeltaFvCLG strain FG31 achieved the highest reported protein content (61.34%), representing a 22.41% increase over the wild-type strain, while ethanol production was significantly restricted with only minimal CO2 generation. Furthermore, the glucose-to-biomass and glucose-to-protein conversion rate of FG31 increased by 44.02% and 76.41%, respectively. The widespread upregulation of nitrogen metabolism pathway genes associated with multiple amino acid synthesis in FG31 was revealed through transcriptomic analysis, while glycolysis pathway genes linked to ethanol synthesis were downregulated. This work provides new insights into enhancing the mycoprotein content and improving the substrate conversion efficiency.

摘要翻译

由Fusarium venenatum生产的真菌蛋白正日益成为一种广泛使用的食用微生物蛋白来源。然而，其发展受到蛋白质含量低、底物转化效率低以及大量副产物（如乙醇）产生的制约，导致显著的碳损失。本研究识别出一个新的遗传靶点FvCLG，其编码含有刀豆素A样凝集素/葡聚糖酶结构域的蛋白质，与细胞壁构建相关。在5升发酵罐中，DeltaFvCLG菌株FG31实现了报道中最高的蛋白质含量（61.34%），较野生型菌株提高了22.41%，同时乙醇产量显著受限，仅产生极少量CO2。此外，FG31的葡萄糖至生物质和葡萄糖至蛋白质的转化率分别提高了44.02%和76.41%。通过转录组分析，揭示了FG31中与多种氨基酸合成相关的氮代谢途径基因广泛上调，而与乙醇合成相关的糖酵解途径基因则下调。这项工作为提升真菌蛋白含量和改善底物转化效率提供了新见解。
