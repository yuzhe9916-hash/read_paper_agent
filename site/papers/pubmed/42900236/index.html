CCKBR signalling controls human definitive endoderm specification and pancreatic lineage differentiation.

Saber, Lama; Kozawa, Kei; Karampelias, Christos; Jing, Changying; Jaki, Jessica; Akgun Canan, Melis; Sterr, Michael; Rodrigues Pereira, Osvaldo; Czarnecki, Oliver; Korbutt, Gregory S; Pepper, Andrew R; Moya, Noel; Burtscher, Ingo; Lickert, Heiko; Bakhti, Mostafa
Diabetologia · 2026-10-20 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究利用CRISPR/Cas9敲除CCKBR研究其在人胰腺分化中的作用，但聚焦于发育信号通路而非核酸酶切割或碱基编辑机制，与本课题无直接关联。

摘要

AIMS/HYPOTHESIS: Paracrine signalling by hormone-producing endocrine cells potentially influences pancreatic development, but this feedback regulation remains poorly understood. Here, we investigated the roles of gastrin (GAST), cholecystokinin (CCK) and their receptor cholecystokinin B receptor (CCKBR) during human pancreatic development. METHODS: Human iPSCs were differentiated stepwise into definitive endoderm and then the pancreatic endocrine lineage. To assess CCKBR function, CRISPR/Cas9 was used to generate CCKBR-knockout iPSC lines. We profiled transcriptional states by bulk and single-cell RNA-seq with computational analyses, and complemented these with biochemical assays, flow cytometry (FACS) and confocal microscopy. RESULTS: CCKBR expression peaked during definitive endoderm formation and progressively declined during differentiation, remaining detectable in pancreatic progenitors but largely absent from the endocrine lineage. By contrast, GAST and CCK were expressed only transiently during pancreatic progenitor and endocrine lineage formation. Pharmacological activation/inhibition and genetic knockout implicated CCKBR signalling during definitive endoderm formation. Consistently, bulk RNA-seq indicated that CCKBR deletion triggered ectodermal- and mesodermal-associated transcriptional programs and diminished pancreatic lineage specification. During pancreatic induction and lineage formation, sustained CCKBR activation with GAST or CCK attenuated endocrinogenesis and promoted epithelial structure formation. Further analysis revealed the emergence of a pancreatic ductal-like cell formation under prolonged CCKBR stimulation. CONCLUSIONS/INTERPRETATION: This study provides mechanistic evidence that the GAST/CCK-CCKBR axis is functionally important during human endoderm and pancreas development. This pathway is repeatedly required during in vitro pluripotent stem cell differentiation, regulating germ-layer formation as well as ductal vs endocrine fate decisions. These findings support the optimisation of pancreatic differentiation protocols and inform future studies on whether this axis contributes to human pancreas development and disease.

摘要翻译

目的/假设：激素生成性内分泌细胞的旁分泌信号可能影响胰腺发育，但这种反馈调节仍知之甚少。在此，我们研究了胃泌素（GAST）、胆囊收缩素（CCK）及其受体胆囊收缩素B受体（CCKBR）在人类胰腺发育过程中的作用。方法：将人类iPSCs逐步分化为定型内胚层，进而分化为胰腺内分泌谱系。为评估CCKBR功能，使用CRISPR/Cas9生成了CCKBR敲除的iPSC系。我们通过批量及单细胞RNA-seq结合计算分析来描绘转录状态，并辅以生化测定、流式细胞术（FACS）和共聚焦显微镜检查。结果：CCKBR表达在定型内胚层形成期间达到峰值，并在分化过程中逐渐下降，在胰腺祖细胞中仍可检测到，但在内分泌谱系中基本缺失。相比之下，GAST和CCK仅在胰腺祖细胞和内分泌谱系形成期间短暂表达。药理学激活/抑制和基因敲除表明CCKBR信号在定型内胚层形成中起作用。一致地，批量RNA-seq显示CCKBR缺失触发了外胚层和中胚层相关的转录程序，并减弱了胰腺谱系特化。在胰腺诱导和谱系形成期间，用GAST或CCK持续激活CCKBR减弱了内分泌发生并促进了上皮结构形成。进一步分析揭示，在长期CCKBR刺激下出现了胰腺导管样细胞形成。结论/解释：本研究提供了机制性证据，表明GAST/CCK-CCKBR轴在人类内胚层和胰腺发育中具有功能重要性。该通路在体外多能干细胞分化过程中反复需要，调节胚层形成以及导管与内分泌命运决定。这些发现支持优化胰腺分化方案，并为未来研究该轴是否参与人类胰腺发育和疾病提供信息。
