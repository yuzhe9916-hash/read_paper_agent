PSK2a is required for cambial development and xylem formation during secondary growth in poplar.

Yue, Songqing; Tian, Dongdong; Ma, Xiao; Qi, Cheng; Wan, Mengjie; Qi, Juan; Cheng, Qian; Tian, Lidan; Shi, Xueping; Zheng, Bo
Plant physiology and biochemistry : PPB · 2026-10-31 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究利用CRISPR/Cas9敲除和过表达探究杨树PSK2a在维管形成层发育中的作用，虽涉及CRISPR工具应用，但核心是植物激素与木材形成，无碱基编辑或交错切割机制研究，与本课题关联微弱。

摘要

Phytosulfokines (PSKs) are sulfated peptide hormones involved in plant growth, development, and stress responses. However, direct genetic evidence for the involvement of PSK peptide in cambium development and secondary vascular development in woody plants remains largely lacking. Here, we identified nine PSK family members in Populus trichocarpa. Phylogenetic, gene structure, conserved motif, and local synteny analyses indicated that the poplar PSK family is evolutionarily conserved, and some members may have arisen from the whole-genome duplication (WGD) event. PSK2a is one of several PSK family genes that are highly expressed in poplar stem vascular tissues, but shows low expression in roots. Subsequently, we generated PtrPSK2a (cloned from P. trichocarpa) overexpression lines in hybrid poplar 717 and CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout lines targeting its endogenous orthologs PtaPSK2a_HAP1 and HAP2hybrid poplar 717. Both gain-of-function and loss-of-function of PSK2a exhibited growth defects, characterized by reduced plant height and stem diameter, with more severe overall growth defects in OE lines. Consistently, histological analysis demonstrated that cambium activity and secondary xylem development were significantly inhibited in both transgenic lines, as reflected by fewer cambium cell layers, narrower xylem, and fewer vessels in young stems. In addition, genes associated with cambial development, vessel differentiation, and secondary cell wall formation were broadly downregulated, accompanied by altered expression of several hormone-related marker genes in transgenic lines. Together, these findings demonstrate that PSK2a is required for normal cambial activity and xylem development during secondary growth in poplar, providing the first direct genetic evidence for PSK function in woody plant vascular development.

摘要翻译

植物磺肽素（Phytosulfokines, PSKs）是参与植物生长、发育和胁迫响应的磺酸化肽类激素。然而，在木本植物中，PSK肽参与形成层发育和次生维管发育的直接遗传学证据仍然 largely 缺乏。在此，我们在毛果杨（Populus trichocarpa）中鉴定了九个PSK家族成员。系统发育、基因结构、保守基序和局部共线性分析表明，杨树PSK家族在进化上保守，部分成员可能起源于全基因组复制（whole-genome duplication, WGD）事件。PSK2a是在杨树茎维管组织中高表达但在根中低表达的若干PSK家族基因之一。随后，我们在杂种杨717中构建了PtrPSK2a（从毛果杨中克隆）过表达株系，以及靶向其内源直系同源基因PtaPSK2a_HAP1和HAP2的CRISPR/Cas9介导的敲除株系。PSK2a的功能获得和功能缺失均表现出生长缺陷，其特征为株高和茎径降低，其中过表达株系的整体生长缺陷更为严重。与此一致，组织学分析表明，两种转基因株系中形成层活性和次生木质部发育均受到显著抑制，表现为幼茎中形成层细胞层数减少、木质部变窄和导管数量减少。此外，与形成层发育、导管分化和次生细胞壁形成相关的基因广泛下调，同时转基因株系中若干激素相关标记基因的表达发生改变。总之，这些发现表明PSK2a是杨树次生生长过程中正常形成层活性和木质部发育所必需的，首次为PSK在木本植物维管发育中的功能提供了直接遗传学证据。
