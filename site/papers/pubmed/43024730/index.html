Noncanonical enhancers control gene expression and cell fate in human pluripotent stem cells.

Cao, Yang; Dovgusha, Oleksandr; Ebrahimi, Ayyub; Cruz, Carmela Rieline V; Bevan, Stephen; Widmer, Lukas; Torabi, Fereshteh; Kurlovich, Julia; Rodriguez-Polo, Ignacio; Khan, Soumen; Patel, Manthan; Shiels, Oliver T; Pradeepa, Madapura M; Flynn, Sean M; Gunesdogan, Ufuk; Schoenfelder, Stefan
Science advances · 2026-11-13 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究通过CRISPR表观编辑和删除鉴定人类多能干细胞中的非经典增强子，虽使用CRISPR工具但聚焦基因调控元件功能，未涉及核酸酶切割、交错断裂或碱基编辑的分子机制，与本课题核心关联较弱。

摘要

Enhancers are key gene regulatory elements that ensure the precise spatiotemporal execution of developmental gene expression programs. However, recent findings indicate that current approaches to identify enhancers may not capture the full repertoire of active enhancers in mammalian genomes. Here, we combine massively parallel enhancer assays with chromatin structure and transcriptome profiling to functionally annotate enhancers genome-wide in human induced pluripotent stem cells. We find that a substantial fraction ( approximately 40%) of accessible chromatin regions with enhancer function lack key features associated with active enhancers, including the active enhancer mark histone H3 lysine-27 acetylation and enhancer-associated RNAs. Perturbation of this class of noncanonical enhancers by CRISPR-mediated epigenome editing or deletion results in decreased levels of target gene expression and, in one instance, loss of pluripotent stem cell characteristics. Collectively, our data demonstrate enhancer activity for a class of gene regulatory elements that had, until now, only been associated with a neutral or inactive status, challenging current models of enhancer function.

摘要翻译

增强子是关键的基因调控元件，确保发育基因表达程序在时空上的精确执行。然而，近期发现表明，当前识别增强子的方法可能无法捕获哺乳动物基因组中全部活性增强子的完整集合。在此，我们结合大规模平行增强子检测与染色质结构及转录组分析，对人类诱导多能干细胞中的全基因组增强子进行功能性注释。我们发现，具有增强子功能的可及染色质区域中，有相当一部分（约40%）缺乏与活性增强子相关的关键特征，包括活性增强子标记组蛋白H3赖氨酸-27乙酰化及增强子相关RNA。通过CRISPR介导的表观基因组编辑或删除对该类非经典增强子进行扰动，导致靶基因表达水平下降，并在一个案例中丧失多能干细胞特性。总体而言，我们的数据证明了一类基因调控元件的增强子活性，而此前这些元件仅与中性或非活性状态相关联，这对当前增强子功能模型提出了挑战。
