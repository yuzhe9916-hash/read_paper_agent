Regulation of the Epstein-Barr Epigenome by Histone Variant MacroH2A.

Castro-Munoz, Leonardo Josue; Yoon, Leena; Karisetty, Bhanu Chandra; Bharti, Aradhana; Tempera, Italo; Soldan, Samantha S; Lieberman, Paul
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-11-20 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究组蛋白变体macroH2A对EBV表观基因组和拷贝数的调控，虽使用CRISPR/Cas9敲除，但核心对象是EBV染色质而非CRISPR/碱基编辑机制，无交错切割或碱基编辑相关分子机制，仅边缘相关。

摘要

Histone variants play an important role in regulating chromatin dynamics of Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV). Here, we investigate the role of histone variant macroH2A in EBV chromatin structure, gene expression and copy number control during latent infection. ChIP-seq analysis in EBV (+) SNU719 gastric carcinoma and Mutu I Burkitt lymphoma cells revealed that macroH2A was most enriched at repetitive DNA elements, including oriLyt, BHLF1, LF3 and terminal repeat (TR) regions of the EBV genome. MacroH2A depletion in EBV-associated gastric cancer cell line SNU719 led to a small increase in some viral lytic genes and increase in viral DNA copy number. RNAseq transcriptomics after macroH2A depletion identified major perturbations in cellular gene pathways, including beta-catenin/TCF signaling and chromatin remodeling. Depletion of macroH2A during EBV lytic reactivation reduced EBV lytic gene activation, but increased EBV DNA copy number. Previous study demonstrated a colocalization and cooperative binding between histone variant H2A.Z and EBNA1. We now show that depletion of macroH2A causes an increase in both H2A.Z and EBNA1 at oriP and cellular binding sites for EBNA1. We also found that gammaH2AX localizes to oriP and that this depends on macroH2A. Finally, we show that CRISPR/Cas9 knockout of macroH2A increases the replication and episome maintenance of oriP-containing plasmids. Taken together, these findings indicate that macroH2A localizes to the repetitive elements of EBV, including oriLyt, and can function to limit H2A.Z and EBNA1 binding at oriP, which alters DNA damage signaling at oriP and limits episomal replication. These findings provide a novel role for macroH2A in maintaining EBV copy number and genome integrity.

摘要翻译

组蛋白变体在调控Epstein-Barr病毒（EBV）的染色质动态中发挥重要作用。在此，我们研究了组蛋白变体macroH2A在潜伏感染期间对EBV染色质结构、基因表达和拷贝数控制的作用。在EBV（+）SNU719胃癌和Mutu I伯基特淋巴瘤细胞中的ChIP-seq分析显示，macroH2A在重复DNA元件中富集最显著，包括EBV基因组的oriLyt、BHLF1、LF3和末端重复（TR）区域。在EBV相关胃癌细胞系SNU719中，macroH2A的耗竭导致部分病毒裂解基因的轻微增加以及病毒DNA拷贝数的增加。macroH2A耗竭后的RNAseq转录组学分析揭示了细胞基因通路的主要扰动，包括β-catenin/TCF信号传导和染色质重塑。在EBV裂解再激活期间，macroH2A的耗竭减少了EBV裂解基因的激活，但增加了EBV DNA拷贝数。先前的研究表明，组蛋白变体H2A.Z与EBNA1之间存在共定位和协同结合。我们现在显示，macroH2A的耗竭导致oriP和EBNA1的细胞结合位点上H2A.Z和EBNA1均增加。我们还发现，gammaH2AX定位于oriP，并且这依赖于macroH2A。最后，我们显示，CRISPR/Cas9敲除macroH2A增加了含oriP质粒的复制和附加体维持。综合这些发现，表明macroH2A定位于EBV的重复元件，包括oriLyt，并能限制H2A.Z和EBNA1在oriP的结合，从而改变oriP处的DNA损伤信号并限制附加体复制。这些发现为macroH2A在维持EBV拷贝数和基因组完整性方面提供了新的作用。
