Disabling COMPASS Promoter Licensing Shifts HDAC-Inhibited Gastric Cancer Toward Sustained Transcriptional Suppression During Recovery.

Song, Jintian; Yu, Hui; Jiang, Jianping; Jiang, Zhihui; Hong, Zhongshi; Zhang, Xu; Chen, Luchuan; Wei, Shenghong; Wang, Yi
Advanced science (Weinheim, Baden-Wurttemberg, Germany) · 2026-10-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究利用CRISPR-Cas9筛选和WDR5靶向干预探究胃癌中HDAC抑制的转录调控，但核心对象是组蛋白修饰与表观遗传缓冲，而非CRISPR/碱基编辑的切割或编辑机制，仅筛选工具与课题弱相关。

摘要

Histone deacetylase inhibitors (HDACi) broadly increase chromatin acetylation but have limited and variable activity in solid tumors. To define the epigenetic buffering axis that sustains promoter output during HDAC inhibition in gastric cancer. We integrated pharmacologic profiling across a molecularly diverse gastric cancer panel with an epigenetic-focused pooled CRISPR-Cas9 screening library, domain-resolved genetic perturbation, structure-guided WIN-pocket targeting, CCK8 time-course proliferation assays, and pulse-washout clonogenic recovery assays. Chromatin and transcriptional consequences were interrogated by assessing COMPASS integrity and chromatin association, promoter-proximal H3K4me3 dynamics, data-derived ATAC-seq accessibility, and transcriptional output. WDR5 abundance and baseline H3K4me3 levels were associated with sensitivity to combined HDAC and WDR5 WIN-pocket inhibition. Multiple orthogonal approaches identified the WDR5 WIN pocket as a key interface supporting chromatin-associated COMPASS and promoter-proximal H3K4me3 under the tested conditions. WIN-pocket disruption reduced chromatin recovery of COMPASS-associated components and attenuated H3K4me3 at selected growth-linked promoters. This produced a state with elevated H3K27 acetylation, attenuated H3K4me3, and reduced transcriptional output; data-derived ATAC-enriched regions retained a centered accessibility signal, although the mean peak amplitude was lowest in the combination. HDAC inhibitor response is conditioned by promoter licensing via the WDR5-COMPASS H3K4 methylation axis.

摘要翻译

组蛋白去乙酰化酶抑制剂（HDACi）广泛增加染色质乙酰化，但在实体瘤中活性有限且变异较大。为定义在胃癌中HDAC抑制期间维持启动子输出的表观遗传缓冲轴，我们整合了跨分子多样性胃癌细胞系的药理学分析、表观遗传聚焦的混合CRISPR-Cas9筛选文库、结构域解析的遗传扰动、结构引导的WIN口袋靶向、CCK8时间过程增殖实验以及脉冲-洗脱克隆形成恢复实验。通过评估COMPASS完整性和染色质关联、启动子近端H3K4me3动态、数据衍生的ATAC-seq可及性以及转录输出，探究了染色质和转录后果。WDR5丰度和基线H3K4me3水平与对HDAC和WDR5 WIN口袋联合抑制的敏感性相关。多种正交方法确定WDR5 WIN口袋为在测试条件下支持染色质相关COMPASS和启动子近端H3K4me3的关键界面。WIN口袋破坏减少了COMPASS相关组分的染色质恢复，并减弱了选定生长相关启动子处的H3K4me3。这产生了一种状态，其特征为H3K27乙酰化升高、H3K4me3减弱以及转录输出降低；数据衍生的ATAC富集区域保留了中心可及性信号，尽管平均峰幅度在联合处理中最低。HDAC抑制剂反应受WDR5-COMPASS H3K4甲基化轴介导的启动子许可调节。
