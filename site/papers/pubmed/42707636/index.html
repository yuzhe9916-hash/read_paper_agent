BioCNT-TLS engineered systemic mobile resistome for sustainable crop protection.

Li, Wenbo; Farooq, Tahir; Hussain, Muhammad Dilshad; Brosnan, Christopher A; Chen, Huanhuan
Frontiers in plant science · 2026-09-08 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文综述了碳纳米管与tRNA样结构结合用于作物保护的RNAi递送系统，不涉及无序蛋白、蛋白互作或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

The global agricultural sector faces an existential threat from viral quasispecies that bypass traditional resistance through high-frequency mutation and rapid systemic colonization. Conventional strategies, including chemical pesticides and transgenic modifications, are increasingly constrained by environmental toxicity, regulatory hurdles, and the biological arms race of viral evasion. This review critically expands upon the transformative potential of biocompatible carbon nanotubes conjugated with tRNA-like structures as a non-transgenic, systemic delivery platform for RNA interference effectors. We analyze the mechanistic synergy between nanomaterial-mediated incorporation and vascular trafficking, proposing a systemic mobile resistome concept, in which BioCNT-TLS complexes may provide transgene-free, RNA-mediated interference distributed across aerial tissues from a single foliar application, an effect demonstrated in a single recent study and not yet validated at the whole-plant or field scale. By synthesizing data on RNAi pyramiding, spray-to-edit CRISPR technologies, and the ecotoxicological footprint of carbon nanomaterials, we provide a blueprint for the future of precision crop protection.

摘要翻译

全球农业部门正面临来自病毒准种（viral quasispecies）的生存威胁，这些准种通过高频突变和快速系统性定殖绕过传统抗性。传统策略，包括化学农药和转基因改造，日益受到环境毒性、监管障碍以及病毒逃逸的生物学军备竞赛的制约。本综述批判性地拓展了与tRNA样结构（tRNA-like structures）偶联的生物相容性碳纳米管（biocompatible carbon nanotubes）作为RNA干扰效应子（RNA interference effectors）的非转基因、系统性递送平台的变革潜力。我们分析了纳米材料介导的整合与维管运输之间的机制协同，提出了系统性移动抗性组（systemic mobile resistome）概念，其中BioCNT-TLS复合物可能通过单次叶面施用，在气生组织中实现无转基因的RNA介导干扰，这一效应在近期一项单一研究中得到展示，但尚未在全植株或田间尺度上得到验证。通过综合RNAi堆叠（RNAi pyramiding）、喷雾编辑CRISPR技术（spray-to-edit CRISPR technologies）以及碳纳米材料生态毒理学足迹的数据，我们为未来精准作物保护提供了蓝图。
