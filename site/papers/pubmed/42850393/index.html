Loss of Nod25 function does not affect nitrogen-fixing symbiosis in Medicago truncatula.

Biro, Janos Barnabas; Domonkos, Agota; Gungor, Berivan; Farkas, Attila; Mohammadi Eghbash, Elmira; Kiss, Gyorgy Botond; Kalo, Peter
Plant molecular biology · 2026-10-09 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文用CRISPR/Cas9敲除蒺藜苜蓿NOD25基因并评估共生表型，虽涉及CRISPR工具应用，但核心是共生固氮功能研究，缺乏对切割/碱基编辑分子机制的探讨，与本课题关联度低。

摘要

The formation of nitrogen-fixing symbiotic nodules is a complex process that involves extensive transcriptional reprogramming of legume roots to enable intracellular accommodation of host compatible rhizobia. This process requires coordinated regulation of hundreds to thousands of genes, including the induction of highly expressed genes, traditionally referred to as nodulin genes. While the functions of many nodulins have been elucidated, a substantial number remained poorly characterized, or their functions are still unclear. One such gene, NOD25, was originally identified based on differential hybridization between roots and nodules and is expressed at exceptionally high levels in Medicago nodules. MtNOD25-like proteins in IRLC legumes exhibit a repetitive modular structure flanked by conserved N- and C-terminal regions, with clade-specific repeat organization. To understand the role of NOD25 in symbiotic nitrogen fixation, we generated loss-of-function mutants in Medicago truncatula using CRISPR/Cas9-mediated genome editing. Four independent Mtnod25 mutant lines displayed normal growth under symbiotic conditions and showed no detectable defects in nodule colonization, symbiosome formation, or bacteroid differentiation. These results indicate that MtNOD25 is not essential for symbiosis in M. truncatula under the conditions tested. However, the biological function of MtNOD25 has not yet been elucidated, and its highly specialized evolutionary conservation suggests that it may have a specific, non-essential role in modulating or fine-tuning symbiotic processes under particular environmental or physiological conditions that remain to be defined.

摘要翻译

固氮共生结瘤的形成是一个复杂的过程，涉及豆科植物根部广泛的转录重编程，以实现对宿主相容性根瘤菌的细胞内容纳。该过程需要协调调控数百至数千个基因，包括诱导高表达基因，这些基因传统上被称为结瘤素基因（nodulin genes）。尽管许多结瘤素的功能已被阐明，但仍有大量结瘤素的特征尚未明确，或其功能仍不清楚。其中一个基因，NOD25，最初通过根与结瘤之间的差异杂交鉴定得到，并在苜蓿（Medicago）结瘤中以异常高的水平表达。IRLC豆科植物中的MtNOD25样蛋白表现出由保守的N端和C端区域侧翼的重复模块化结构，并具有进化枝特异性的重复组织。为了理解NOD25在共生固氮中的作用，我们利用CRISPR/Cas9介导的基因组编辑在蒺藜苜蓿（Medicago truncatula）中生成了功能缺失突变体。四个独立的Mtnod25突变体品系在共生条件下表现出正常生长，并且在结瘤定殖、共生体形成或类菌体分化方面未检测到明显缺陷。这些结果表明，在测试条件下，MtNOD25在蒺藜苜蓿共生中并非必需。然而，MtNOD25的生物学功能尚未阐明，其高度特化的进化保守性提示，它可能在特定环境或生理条件下，对调节或微调共生过程具有特定的非必需作用，这些条件仍有待确定。
