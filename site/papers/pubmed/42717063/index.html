Architecture of a DNA-guided Cas12a.

Ocampo, Rodrigo Fregoso; Orosco, Carlos; Huang, Boyu; West, Madeline S; Parker, Bay; Jain, Piyush K; Taylor, David W
Nature structural & molecular biology · 2026-09-10 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究Cas12a与DNA引导RNA靶标复合物的冷冻电镜结构，对象为CRISPR核酸酶而非无序蛋白，且不涉及PPI或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题关联极弱。

摘要

CRISPR-Cas systems have largely been restricted to RNA-guided nucleases. Here, we present the cryo-electron microscopy structure of Acidaminococcus sp. Cas12a bound to a pseudo-DNA (PsiDNA) guide and RNA target, revealing how Cas12a can accomplish DNA-guided RNA recognition. The PsiDNA hairpin bridges the recognition and nuclease lobes, mimicking a PAM-proximal duplex and positioning the spacer to allow formation of a canonical RNA-DNA heteroduplex along the recognition lobe. This provides a structural framework for its activity and provides a potential blueprint for future engineering.

摘要翻译

CRISPR-Cas系统在很大程度上局限于RNA引导的核酸酶。在此，我们展示了Acidaminococcus sp. Cas12a与伪DNA（PsiDNA）引导链和RNA靶标结合的冷冻电镜结构，揭示了Cas12a如何实现DNA引导的RNA识别。PsiDNA发夹结构桥接了识别叶和核酸酶叶，模拟了PAM近端双链体，并将间隔区定位，使得沿识别叶能够形成经典的RNA-DNA异源双链体。这为其活性提供了结构框架，并为未来的工程改造提供了潜在蓝图。
